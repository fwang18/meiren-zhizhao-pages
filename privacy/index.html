Meiren Zhizhao Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Meiren Zhizhao respects and protects user privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Information We Process

The app does not require registration or login and does not ask for your name, phone number, or email address. When you use the chat feature, we process the chat content that you choose to enter.

2. How We Use Information

Chat content is used only to generate responses, provide app functionality, troubleshoot problems, and maintain service security. It is not used to train models, for advertising or marketing, or for tracking across apps.

3. Service Providers

To generate responses, chat content is transmitted through Tencent CloudBase to FastGPT. These service providers process relevant information only to provide and maintain app functionality.

4. Retention

Chat content is generally retained for approximately 30 days and then deleted, unless longer retention is required by law or necessary to address a security issue.

5. Data Association and Security

Chat content is not linked to your real-world identity. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no network transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit chat content. To ask a question, request deletion of relevant information, or exercise another privacy right, contact us using the email below.
